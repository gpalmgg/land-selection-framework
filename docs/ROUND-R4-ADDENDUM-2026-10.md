## What did not change

- **Counters:** still v1, r4. No file was renamed. The group has not picked up the r4 to r5 handoff.
- **Committed decisions 1 to 9:** untouched. The source-docs only gained clearly attributed additions; a script compares them with the pinned r4 baseline line by line.
- **No scoring:** no composite, no weights, no ranking, and no label that ranks one region over another. Threshold filters filter; the 4 categorical qualitative filters filter; display order is not a ranking. Land standing and the legal pathway are never scored and never a numeric filter.
- **Native units, source and vintage on every value;** state and trajectory per criterion where the data allows.
- **Framing:** a bioregioning tool for communities seeking to belong to a place and help it flourish over fifty to a hundred years, with reciprocity as the spine. Where arriving in a place would harm the community already there, the honest answer is not to go.

## What the group is asked

Read the amended Handoff Request in the Overview. Two decisions (host-community standing, and where the honest answer is not to go) need explicit assent and do not follow the 5-working-day default. The slate-admission rule asks the group to ratify, amend or reverse it.
