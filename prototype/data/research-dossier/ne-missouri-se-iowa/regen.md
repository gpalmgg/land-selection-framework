# Regenerative Network — Northeast Missouri / Southeast Iowa, United States

**Headline finding:** Four named, placeable communities sit within 100 km of the region point, all in northeast Missouri and all long-rooted: Sandhill Farm (1974), Dancing Rabbit Ecovillage (1997), Red Earth Farms (2005), Bear Creek Community Land Trust (2014/2018). The network is small and slow by design.

**Key data point (with vintage):** 4 sites within 100 km of [-92.0, 40.5], counted only where GEN, FIC or OSM lists them, retrieved 2026-10-04: Dancing Rabbit (FIC, GEN profile, OSM node 3644771010), Sandhill Farm (FIC, OSM node 151348387 named "Sand Hill", place=hamlet, community=intentional, 23.6 km from the point), Red Earth Farms (FIC), Bear Creek Community Land Trust (FIC).

**Supporting facts:**
- Dancing Rabbit (FIC): founded 1993, 280 acres bought 1997, about 65 people and 39 members on the FIC listing; GEN profile gives founding year 1997. Residents sign ecological covenants (no personal motor vehicles on the land, sustainable electricity, organic farming standards, reclaimed or sustainably harvested lumber). Land is held by Dancing Rabbit Land Trust (a 501(c)(2) partnered with a 501(c)(3)); members lease a "warren" and own improvements, not land.
- Sandhill Farm (FIC): established 1974, 4 adults and 2 youth, restructured in 2019 from income-sharing to private dwellings with collective land stewardship; membership "intentionally slow" at 18-24 months.
- Red Earth Farms (FIC): 76 acres bought 2005 adjacent to Dancing Rabbit, homesteads on autonomous leaseholds, six members listed.
- Bear Creek Community Land Trust (FIC): 184 acres, mission "uplift the health of people and the earth", leases valid for a lifetime or 99 years, each member submits a Land Use Plan reviewed by the others. Sandhill's FIC page puts it "approximately 35 miles away in the LaPlata area" (about 56 km from Sandhill, so inside 100 km of the point).
- Listed inside Bear Creek and not counted separately: White Rose Catholic Worker Farm (since 2014 in northeast Missouri, 6 miles from La Plata) and Carya Grove (an 11-acre sub-community).
- Not counted: The Possibility Alliance (La Plata, 2007; named on Dancing Rabbit's nearby-communities page, no FIC/GEN/OSM listing found; its FIC slug returned 404); Emberfield Heart Collective Ecovillage and Dandelion (FIC listings, location given only as "Missouri"); Prairie Hill Cohousing and River City Housing Collective (FIC, Iowa; Prairie Hill sits about 133 km away per OSM snapshot).
- Fairfield, Iowa (Jefferson County) is inside the 100 km radius but outside the footprint; no FIC or OSM community there was found by keyword search, which is not an exhaustive geographic query.
- Live OSM query (Overpass, 2026-10-04) for community=intentional, community=ecovillage, place=ecovillage and intentional_community within 100 km returned only Sand Hill; the broader name-regex query timed out.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** This is a floor. Both FIC and OSM under-list quiet rural projects. The point of the number is that a handful of long-lived communities already hold the relationships here; a newcomer would be a guest of them, not a founder.
