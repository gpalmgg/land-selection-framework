# Stability (Conflict) — Northeast Missouri / Southeast Iowa, United States

**Headline finding:** No UCDP georeferenced conflict event is recorded within 200 km of the region point in any year from 1989 to 2024.

**Key data point (with vintage):** UCDP GED v25.1 (385,918 events, 1989-2024), haversine distance from 40.5 N, -92.0 W: 0 events within 200 km, 250 km or 300 km for 2019-2024; 0 events within 200 km in all years. Computed 2026-10-04 (tools/conflict.py). The 2025 year appears only in later GED releases (v26.1): not checked.

**Supporting facts:**
- A 200 km circle from the region point reaches Illinois and southern Iowa beyond the footprint, including Jefferson County, Iowa (Fairfield), which is outside the footprint but inside the radius.
- UCDP counts organised violence with at least one fatality; it does not capture civil unrest or political tension in the United States.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** The metric is a floor for armed-conflict proximity, nothing more.
