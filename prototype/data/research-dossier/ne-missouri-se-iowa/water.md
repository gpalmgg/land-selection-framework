# Water — Northeast Missouri / Southeast Iowa, United States

**Headline finding:** Projected baseline water stress is low across the footprint (area-weighted 0.019 on the Aqueduct 2050 business-as-usual ratio), and two state regimes govern use: Iowa requires a permit above 25,000 gallons per day and can restrict uses in a set order in a shortage; Missouri asks major users (100,000 gallons per day) to register.

**Key data point (with vintage):** WRI Aqueduct 4.0 bau50_ws_x_r, area-weighted over the seven-county footprint (8,572.6 km2, five HydroBASINS level-6 basins): 0.0192; basin values 0.0201 (742830, 3,908 km2), 0.0109 (742841, 2,292 km2), 0.0036 (742917, 1,294 km2), 0.0521 (742850, 1,071 km2), 0.1136 (742918, 8.2 km2, Low-medium). Category by area: Low (<10%) 8,564 km2, Low-medium 8 km2. Baseline bws_raw area-weighted 0.0144. Computed 2026-10-04 (tools/aqueduct.py on the Aqueduct 4.0 geodatabase).

**Supporting facts:**
- Iowa Code 455B.268: a permit is required to divert, store or withdraw water from any surface or groundwater source except a nonregulated use; 455B.261(11) defines nonregulated use as under 25,000 gallons per day. (Iowa Code 2026, ch. 455B, opened.)
- Iowa Code 455B.266 priority allocation: after a local-shortage petition, drought forecast, governor's proclamation or local crisis, the department may restrict uses, even of permit holders and nonregulated users, in this order: water conveyed across state lines; recreational or aesthetic use; irrigation of hay, corn, soybeans, oats, grain sorghum or wheat; irrigation of other crops; manufacturing.
- RSMo 256.400 and 256.410: a major water user (100,000 gallons per day or more from any stream, river, lake, well, spring) must file a registration with the Division of Geology and Land Survey. Missouri well and riparian rules were not opened.
- Rainfall catchment is part of Dancing Rabbit's published practice (dancingrabbit.org rainwater page listed).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** Low modelled stress is a basin-scale ratio; it says nothing about a parcel's well, spring or pond. Rathbun Regional Water Association (named in the brief) could not be verified: rrwa.com is a parked domain.
