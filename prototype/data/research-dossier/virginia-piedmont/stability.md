# Stability: Virginia Piedmont (Louisa, Fluvanna and Orange), USA

**Headline finding:** No fatal organised-violence events within 200 km of the region point in 2019-2024. The nearest events in the entire UCDP record are the Pentagon attack of 2001 (136.6 km, 189 deaths) and, in the newer v26.1 release, two single-fatality events in 2016 (Lynchburg, Virginia 108.6 km; Gaithersburg, Maryland 154.0 km). The 200 km circle reaches the Washington DC metro, which is why the count was computed rather than assumed.

**Key data point (with vintage):** UCDP GED v25.1 (385,918 events, 1989-2024), events within 200 km (haversine) of 38.0 N, -78.2 with year 2019-2024: **0**. Radii 250 km and 300 km also 0. UCDP GED v26.1 (417,968 events, 1989-2025): also 0 in 2019-2024 and 0 in 2025. All years within 200 km: v25.1 one event (2001); v26.1 three events (2001; two in 2016). Computed 2026-10-04 from the repo's GEDEvent_v25_1.csv and the v26.1 CSV in the millevaches working folder.

**Supporting facts:**
- The earthquake of 23 August 2011 (M5.8, centred in Louisa County about five miles south-southwest of Mineral, depth about 3.7 miles) caused over $70 million of damage in Louisa County, $57.5 million of it to public school structures (Wikipedia, Louisa County).
- North Anna Nuclear Generating Station is in Louisa County; its two reactors shut down automatically in the 2011 earthquake (Wikipedia, opened).
- Fast growth rather than instability: see accessibility.md.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** The stability risks here are physical (rare moderate earthquakes, a nuclear plant on a reservoir, storm weather), not political. Neither is a reason to stay away; both belong in building and emergency plans.
