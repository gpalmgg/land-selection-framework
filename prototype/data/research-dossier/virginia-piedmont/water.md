# Water: Virginia Piedmont (Louisa, Fluvanna and Orange), USA

**Headline finding:** WRI Aqueduct 4.0 puts the footprint at "Medium-high (20-40%)" baseline water stress on 83 percent of its area and "High (40-80%)" on 17 percent by 2050 (business as usual). The area-weighted ratio is 0.27. This is a basin ratio of withdrawals to supply, driven by regional demand (including the Richmond and Charlottesville metros and power cooling), not a statement that rural wells here run dry.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Aqueduct 4.0 `bau50_ws_x_r` (2050 BAU, raw 0-1 ratio), area-weighted over four HydroBASINS level-6 basins that cover the footprint exactly (2,964.3 km2): basin 731904, 0.200 (1,631.4 km2, Medium-high); basin 731906, 0.313 (821.8 km2, Medium-high); basin 731902, 0.415 (497.3 km2, High); basin 731903, 0.455 (13.8 km2, High). **Area-weighted 0.269.** The basin at 38.0 N, -78.2 is 731904 (0.200). Aqueduct baseline `bws_raw` area-weighted 0.209. Aqueduct 4.0 download (Y2023 M07 D05), computed 2026-10-04.

**Supporting facts:**
- Lake Anna (Louisa, Spotsylvania and Orange) is a reservoir of about 20 sq mi and 200 miles of shoreline, impounding the North Anna River; Virginia Electric and Power (Dominion Energy) owns it and it exists to supply cooling water to the North Anna Nuclear Generating Station (Wikipedia, opened). About 120 communities surround it.
- The James and Rivanna rivers meet at Point of Fork in Fluvanna, the site of Rassawek (see legal.md); the James River Water Authority voted on 16 March 2022 to relocate its planned pump-station project away from Rassawek (Cultural Heritage Partners, opened).
- Virginia Code Title 62.1: Virginia Water Protection Permits may condition withdrawals, and "domestic and other existing beneficial uses shall be considered the highest priority" (62.1-44.15:22; 62.1-242). An emergency permit can be issued for a drought-driven public supply shortfall (62.1-44.15:22 C).
- Drainage: North and South Anna to the Pamunkey and York; Rivanna to the James; Rapidan to the Rappahannock. All reach Chesapeake Bay.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** A Medium-high basin ratio near a power reservoir and two growing metros means the water story is allocation and growth, not scarcity today. Check the well record, the stream's drought flow and the neighbouring withdrawals before any building; Virginia DEQ withdrawal data was not reachable from this run (HTTP 403).
