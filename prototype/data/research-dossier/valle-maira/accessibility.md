# Accessibility and population — Valle Maira, Italy

**Headline finding:** about 11,500 people live in the 13 comuni (20 per km²), but 61 percent of them live in Dronero at the valley mouth; the 11 mountain comuni together hold about 2,900 people (5.9 per km²) and no hospital is inside the valley.

**Key data point (with vintage):** JRC GHSL GHS-POP R2023A, 1 km Mollweide, 2030 projection, sum over footprint pixels with centres inside the footprint: 10,579 people / 568.8 km² = **18.6 persons per km²** (2020 layer: 11,352 / 568.8 = 20.0). Official ISTAT resident population on 1 January 2025 (POSAS_2025): **11,458 people, 20.1 per km²** over the same polygons (area from OpenStreetMap boundaries, EPSG:3035: 568.8 km²). Mountain 11-comune sub-aggregate (489.8 km²): GHSL 2030 6.3 per km²; ISTAT 2025 2,892 people, 5.9 per km². Computed 2026-10-04.

**Supporting facts:**
- ISTAT 1 Jan 2025 by comune: Dronero 7,024; Villar San Costanzo 1,542; Roccabruna 1,533; San Damiano Macra 409; Prazzo 175; Acceglio 164; Cartignano 161; Stroppo 103; Celle di Macra 88; Canosio 80; Elva 79; Marmora 58; Macra 42. Pettenati (2013) gives 2011 figures Macra 52, Canosio 82, Dronero 7,205.
- Trend 2019 to 2025 (ISTAT POSAS, 1 January): footprint 11,555 to 11,458 (−0.8 percent); mountain 11: 2,953 to 2,892 (−2.1 percent).
- No OSM hospital point lies inside the footprint. Emergency-tagged hospitals, straight-line from [7.1, 44.45]: Ospedale Santa Croce (Cuneo) 35.5 km, Ospedale Civile di Saluzzo 37.9 km. Nearest tagged hospital points: Hôpital Saint-Maur 25.7 km (a French-named OSM point), Ospedale di Caraglio (emergency=no) 26.0 km. Road distance from the high valley is much longer than the straight line.
- Services the newcomers interviewed by Pettenati named as the minimum: primary and secondary school, a food shop, and internet access (opened by the standing task).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** the 20 per km² average hides two valleys: a small town at the mouth and a very thin upper valley. The slider's single number should be read as a floor on the upper communes' emptiness, not a density anyone lives at.
