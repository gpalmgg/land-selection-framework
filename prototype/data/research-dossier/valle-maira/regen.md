# Regenerative network — Valle Maira, Italy

**Headline finding:** the national ecovillage network RIVE (the Italian network of the Global Ecovillage Network) lists four ecovillages within 100 km of the valley, none inside it; OpenStreetMap lists none under the ecovillage and intentional-community tags. The valley's own newcomer culture (organic farming, slow tourism, telework) is real but is not an ecovillage network.

**Key data point (with vintage):** RIVE "Ecovillaggi RIVE" member list (ecovillaggi.it, opened 2026-10-04), entries within 100 km of [7.1, 44.45] (haversine; coordinates from the listing GPS where given, otherwise municipality geocode via Nominatim): **4**.
1. Germogli Cuneo, Boves (CN), 40.0 km — "ecovillaggio in costruzione".
2. La casa rotta, Cherasco (CN), 66.0 km — "il primo ecovillaggio nelle Langhe", 7 adults and 6 children living together per its entry (GPS 44.6247, 7.8960).
3. Torri Superiore, Ventimiglia (IM), 76.9 km — active since 1989, 25 residents per its entry (GPS 43.8396, 7.5540).
4. Go wild families, Taggia (IM), 87.3 km (municipality geocode, ±5 km) — "ecovillaggio in costruzione".
OpenStreetMap, Overpass snapshot 2026-10-04T17:56Z, same tag set as the site's ecovillage layer (community=intentional/ecovillage, place/amenity=ecovillage, intentional_community, GEN network tags, permaculture tags) within 100 km: **0 elements**.

**Supporting facts:**
- Not counted (other RIVE categories or outside range): L'Altrove, Narzole (63.1 km; listed under "Progetti RIVE", a nascent community); Cohousing Montesole, Montaldo Torinese (90.5 km; listed under Cohousing); Roseto dei Papi, Savona (110 km, out of range); Damanhur (118 km, out); TerraAmica, Issiglio (122 km, out); Orosia, Bollengo (131 km, out).
- Tested and NOT counted because neither GEN/RIVE nor the ecovillage OSM tags list them: Borgata Paraloup, Rittana (Valle Stura, 24 km; run by a community agricultural cooperative; OSM carries it as place=hamlet and a community_centre); Associazione Dislivelli (a Turin research and communication association, not a site); Ostana's Borgata Villa (Valle Po) and Chambra d'Oc were not found in either list.
- OSM name search (ecovillagg*, cohousing, colieu, permacultur*, Paraloup, Dislivelli, Chambra d*, Borgata Villa) in the box 44.0–45.0°N, 6.3–7.9°E returned only Paraloup (hamlet), Rifugio Paraloup (community_centre, operator Germinale cooperativa) and Borgata Villar (a hamlet).
- GEN's own directory (ecovillage.org, 3,190 results, Italian-language filter shows 16) could not be queried programmatically; the RIVE list is the Italian GEN network's member list.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** a count of 4, two of them still "in construction" and all in the neighbouring plain or the Ligurian coast, says the valley's reciprocity is with the communes and the Occitan and alpeggio culture, not with an ecovillage scene. Programmes and support structures (Dislivelli, the Consorzio Turistico) are not sites and are not counted.
