# Legal + Economic — Valle Maira, Italy

**Headline finding:** Italian land law lets a foreign buyer hold freehold subject to a reciprocity rule that EU citizens and most residents are exempt from, and the real gate in the valley is not who may buy but what the parcel is: collective land (usi civici, demanio civico) is inalienable and permanently agro-silvo-pastoral, farm tenants and farming neighbours have a statutory first claim, and any building needs a permit under the commune's own plan. Statutory farmland values are low (EUR 1,394 to 18,477 per ha across pasture to irrigated meadow, 2025), but they are expropriation tariffs, not asking prices, and borgata house prices were not retrieved.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Agenzia delle Entrate, Valori Agricoli Medi (VAM) 2025, Ufficio provinciale di Cuneo (Commissione provinciale n. 1 of 13 January 2025, table dated 23 June 2025), EUR per ha. Regione agraria 2 "Alte valli Maira e Grana" (Acceglio, Canosio, Celle di Macra, Elva, Macra, Marmora, Prazzo, Stroppo, with Castelmagno and Pradleves): pascolo 1,394; bosco d'alto fusto 2,546; prato 4,325; seminativo 5,004; castagneto 5,757; prato irriguo 9,084; vigneto 7,636. Regione agraria 3 "Montagna delle medie valli Maira e Gesso" (Cartignano, Dronero, Roccabruna, San Damiano Macra, Villar San Costanzo, with Montemale, Monterosso Grana, Valgrana): pascolo 1,394; bosco d'alto fusto 2,666; prato 8,959; seminativo 8,959; castagneto 6,787; prato irriguo 18,477; seminativo irriguo 21,565. Confidence: MEDIUM (official table, but a statutory compensation value, not market transactions).

**Supporting facts:**
- Foreign ownership: Art. 16 of the Disposizioni sulla legge in generale (Preleggi) admits foreigners to the civil rights of citizens "a condizione di reciprocità" (text read on Brocardi; the Normattiva article page does not render). D.Lgs. 286/1998 art. 2 c.2 (opened on Normattiva) gives regularly resident foreigners the civil rights of Italian citizens, with reciprocity kept for certain rights; the exemption list (EU/EEA citizens, holders of work, family, humanitarian and study permits) comes from the Brocardi summary and is UNVERIFIED against primary text. No Piedmont-specific foreign-buyer rule was found. Frontier-zone rules were not checked.
- Collective land: Law 168/2017 art. 3 c.1 lists beni collettivi (terre of the inhabitants of a comune or frazione, uses civici not yet liquidated, water bodies on which residents exercise usi civici); c.3: "inalienabilità, indivisibilità, inusucapibilità e perpetua destinazione agro-silvo-pastorale"; c.5: use follows the collective's own rules. Art. 2 c.4 (opened by the standing task): administered by the collectivity's bodies, otherwise by the comune with separate administration. Which parcels are collective is per comune and was not looked up.
- First claim on farmland sales: tenant farmers who have cultivated the let plot for the statutory period (Law 590/1965 art. 8, period cut to two years by Law 817/1971 art. 7) have a pre-emption right, with a 30-day window and a one-year redemption right if the owner skips notice; art. 8 does not apply where the plan zones the land for building, industrial or tourist use. Law 817/1971 art. 7 extends the right to coltivatori diretti and professional agricultural entrepreneurs owning adjoining land, provided no tenant is on the plot.
- Building: new construction and certain restructurings and changes of use need a permesso di costruire (DPR 380/2001 art. 10, opened). Zoning and what a borgata building may become is set by each comune's PRGC; the Piedmont law on recovery of rustici (LR 9/2003) was abrogated by LR 16/2018 art. 17 (Arianna page opened); the current regional rules were not opened.
- Language minority: Law 482/1999 art. 2 protects Occitan; the Regione Piemonte list of comuni that applied the law (PDF, 2018) names all 13 comuni of the Unione under "minoranza occitana, Provincia di Cuneo" and does not name Busca; it says the comuni "hanno deliberato" their own belonging and the provincial councils confirm the territorial scope.
- VAM trend, same regione agraria 2: prato 3,922 (2021), 4,159 (2023), 4,325 (2024 and 2025); pascolo 1,263 (2021), 1,340 (2023), 1,394 (2024 and 2025). Regione agraria 3 prato: 8,614 (2023), 8,959 (2024 and 2025). Roughly +10 percent 2021 to 2025 in regione agraria 2.
- Inner-areas strategy: the Unione's SNAI page names the "Valli Maira e Grana" area, EUR 2.44 million of regional rural-development funds (PSR operation 16.7.1) and borgata community spaces; its stated cross-cutting aim includes attracting new firms and workers to the area (mountain belt in particular), but it contains no invitation to settle in any particular borgata (re-read by the verify pass 2026-10-04).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** do not read VAM as a price list. Buying "land" in the high valley usually means buying a bundle of small fragmented parcels beside collective land the collectivity or comune administers; a borgata building comes with its own questions (heirs, plan zoning, access, water). Where a borgata has not been offered for reuse by its heirs or its comune, the honest answer is not there. Second homes are the harm the valley's own tourism consortium frames as "non è una meta da consumare".
