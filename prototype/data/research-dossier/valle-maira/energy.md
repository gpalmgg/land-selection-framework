# Energy — Valle Maira, Italy

**Headline finding:** photovoltaic yield is good but varies by about 600 kWh/kWp across the valley with slope and aspect; the footprint grid mean is 1,367 kWh/kWp, the point at [7.1, 44.45] is lower (1,258).

**Key data point (with vintage):** Global Solar Atlas (Solargis) long-term-average PVOUT, API `lta`, sampled on a 0.02° x 0.03° grid inside the footprint (107 points): **mean 1,367 kWh/kWp, median 1,384, min 1,005, max 1,625, p10–p90 1,184–1,537.** The point at the coordinates (ELE 1,348 m): PVOUT 1,258, GHI 1,301 kWh/m². Mountain 11-comune sub-aggregate (93 points): mean 1,370, median 1,383. Retrieved 2026-10-04. GSA metadata: PVOUT version 2.2.68, data period to 2025, layers updated 2026-04-01.

**Supporting facts:**
- By sampled elevation: below 1,000 m 1,366 (19 points); 1,000–1,500 m 1,282 (24); 500–1,500 m 1,319 (43); 1,500–2,200 m 1,353 (38); above 2,200 m 1,468 (26). Correlation of PVOUT with elevation is weak and positive (0.42); the lowest values are shaded north-facing and deep-valley cells.
- Sampled elevations 543–2,923 m (mean 1,684 m).
- The brief's warning stands: the 1,445 figure quoted for another judge's Apennine point does not apply; this is computed on the valley.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** the grid mean weights the whole footprint, including empty high slopes. A roof on a sunny-slope borgata at 1,000–1,400 m and a roof in a shaded side valley differ by several hundred kWh/kWp; check the specific site (and winter shading by ridges), not the mean.
