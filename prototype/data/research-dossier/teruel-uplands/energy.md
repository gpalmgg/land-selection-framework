# Energy — Teruel uplands and the Maestrazgo, Spain (Aragon)

**Headline finding:** Solar potential is excellent and even across the footprint (PVOUT mean 1,628 kWh/kWp per year), and that is exactly why wind and solar siting is contested here.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Global Solar Atlas LTA API (data v1.7, period to 2025, layer updated 2026-04-01), PVOUT_csi at 235 grid points (0.06 x 0.085 degrees) inside the footprint, retrieved 2026-10-04: **mean 1,628, median 1,633, p10-p90 1,577-1,669, min 1,502, max 1,695 kWh/kWp**. At the marker (40.5, -1.2): 1,687 (judge E: 1,667 at 40.4, -1.1). Terrain elevation of the points 677-1,936 m.

**Supporting facts:**
- Siting is a live dispute (sources opened by the standing agent, opinion and campaign voices): a Teruel Existe march against 100 MW plants split into fragments, 25 April 2021 (Eco de Teruel, 16 April 2021); a 2022 open letter from the mayor of Rodenas on wind projects in the Comunidad de Albarracin whose land and income were shifted among villages (Eco de Teruel, 1 Sep 2022).
- A household-scale system is not what those disputes are about; a new resident should expect neighbours to ask what a project is for and who it pays.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** The resource is not the constraint; consent is. Rooftop and village-scale use is welcome in principle; anything on montes of the Comunidad or the municipality goes through the owner.
