# Stability — Teruel uplands and the Maestrazgo, Spain (Aragon)

**Headline finding:** No armed-conflict events in or near the footprint. The instability that matters is slow: depopulation, ageing and housing scarcity, then wildfire and drought.

**Key data point (with vintage):** UCDP GED v25.1 (385,918 events, 1989-2024): **0 events within 200 km of (40.5, -1.2) in 2019-2024** (1 at 250 km and 1 at 300 km, outside the window). GED v26.1 (to 2025): also 0. Only three events in any year within 200 km: Zaragoza 2001 (130 km), Valencia 1992 and 1995 (135 km). Computed 2026-10-04.

**Supporting facts:**
- Population 86,954 (2011) to 83,172 (1 Jan 2025): -4.3%; outside Teruel city -10.0%; since 2021 +2.0%. 85 of 180 municipalities under 100 inhabitants (INE Padron, table 2899).
- Mean age 46.7; 24.6% aged 65 or more; 13.1% foreign nationals (IAEST fichas, INE annual census 1 Jan 2025, population-weighted).
- A rural housing shortage holds back newcomers and young locals, with houses of unknown ownership and a tension between tourism and housing (Eco de Teruel, 30 Sep 2026, announcing a workshop at Tramacastilla on 7 Oct 2026).
- Wildfire: Aliaga 2009, 7,301 ha (see soil.md).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** Stability here means the school staying open. The Griegos school has six pupils and one teacher (its own town hall site, undated); arrival that fills a classroom is read very differently from arrival that buys a second home.
