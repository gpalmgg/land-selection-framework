# Accessibility and population — Teruel uplands and the Maestrazgo, Spain (Aragon)

**Headline finding:** Single-digit density, and almost every municipality is within 60 km (geodesic) of a hospital point, with Teruel city as the hub. The marker sits about 20 km from it.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Footprint of 180 municipalities, 11,089.8 km2 (IGN areas via es.wikipedia; 11,089.7 from GADM polygons), population **83,172** (INE Padron cifras oficiales, 1 Jan 2025, table 2899) = **7.50 p/km2**; outside Teruel city (36,655; 440.4 km2) 46,517 over 10,649.4 km2 = **4.37**. GHSL POP R2023A: 2030 70,900 = 6.39 p/km2 (3.90 outside Teruel city); 2020 76,187 = 6.86. Province: 136,197 over 14,826.98 km2 = 9.19 p/km2, 236 municipalities (the brief's 136,091 is the es.wikipedia figure).

**Supporting facts:**
- By comarca (INE 2025): Comunidad de Teruel 46 municipalities, 47,084 people; Jiloca 40, 12,716; Gudar-Javalambre 24, 7,952; Cuencas Mineras 30, 7,795; Sierra de Albarracin 25, 4,537; Maestrazgo 15, 3,088. Largest: Teruel 36,655, Calamocha 4,655, Utrillas 3,069, Cella 2,702, Monreal del Campo 2,578, Mora de Rubielos 1,752. Median municipality 110 inhabitants; 85 under 100, 158 under 500.
- The IAEST count of municipalities per comarca (fichas) is 46, 40, 30, 25, 24, 15: 180.
- Hospital proximity (OSM snapshot, geodesic, not road time): nearest point from the marker 18.4 km (Hospital San Jose, Teruel); Hospital Obispo Polanco 20.2 km; 4 points within 50 km and 15 within 100 km, but the snapshot includes non-hospitals (rehabilitation centre, clinic, residence, mortuary). Across the 180 municipality centroids: 164 within 50 km (72,954 people), median 33.7 km, farthest 59.3 km (Caminreal); all within 75 km. Mountain roads are slower than the geodesic.
- Distances from the marker: Zaragoza 130 km, Valencia 134 km, Madrid 212 km (geodesic).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** "Within 60 km" is a map fact. In winter on Gudar or Maestrazgo roads it is longer; ask the town hall about the health centre and the school transport before anything else.
