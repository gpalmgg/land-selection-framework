# Regenerative network — Teruel uplands and the Maestrazgo, Spain (Aragon)

**Headline finding:** The regenerative-community network is almost absent inside 100 km: one listed community, whose location could not be confirmed on its own site. The repopulation effort here is run by municipalities, local action groups and programmes, which are support structures, not sites.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Living Atlas export (Heart of Ecovillages, CC BY-SA 4.0, generated 2026-10-02, 6,701 mapped points) within 100 km of (40.5, -1.2): **1 = GAYA community of consciousness, Molinos (Maestrazgo), 72.6 km, verified-active, FIC directory listing (precision "place")**. OSM via Overpass on 2026-10-04: 0 elements for community=intentional/ecovillage, place=ecovillage, permaculture and intentional_community tags within 100 km; 0 for a name regex (ecoaldea, ecovillage, cohousing, permacultura, comunidad intencional, aldea ecol) in the box 39.6-41.4N, 2.3W-0.1W.

**Supporting facts:**
- Just outside the radius: Aldea de Olla (Marines, Valencia; 103.8 km, plus a duplicate listing at 106 km), Familia Feliz (113.6 km and 125 km); transition initiative POR TI+ (Valencia, 139 km, network-verified).
- El Calabacino: Atlas coordinates 37.88, -6.68, about 400 km from the marker. Not within 100 km; not counted.
- GEN's own directory (ecovillage.org/map) is a JavaScript search that could not be queried here; the Atlas ingests the GEN and FIC listings, so it is the substitute.
- Institutions and programmes in the footprint (not counted as sites): Bolsa de Repoblacion (A.D. Serrania Celtiberica; most 2025 entries are outside Teruel; Griegos is the in-footprint entry), Pueblos Vivos Aragon (covers 3 of the 6 comarcas), ASIADER (Sierra de Albarracin), Comunidad de Albarracin (commons).
- HabitaTE (pueblosvivosaragon.com/habitate) is a completed 2006-2012 platform, not a current scheme.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** A newcomer should expect no existing ecovillage to join in the footprint. The partners are the town hall, the local action group and a farming neighbour.
