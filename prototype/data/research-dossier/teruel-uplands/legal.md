# Legal + Economic — Teruel uplands and the Maestrazgo, Spain (Aragon)

**Headline finding:** Anyone may buy land (no nationality bar inland), but the planning rules close the door to a new multi-household settlement on non-urban land and open it to rehabilitating old village buildings; relatives hold a first claim on family rustic land. Province farmland prices are low (a 2,523 EUR/ha weighted mean against Spain's 10,248), which measures rural emptying and is not an invitation: the way in is a municipality's own housing, rented first.

**Key data point (with vintage):** MAPA, Precios medios anuales de las tierras de uso agrario 2024 (definitive; table 20): Teruel province **pastizal 1,508 EUR/ha, tierra arable secano 2,692, olivar secano 2,920, frutos secos secano 3,035, arable regadio 7,592, frutos secos regadio 7,300, olivar regadio 8,090, frutal carnoso regadio 12,682**; area-weighted mean **2,523 EUR/ha** (our arithmetic with the base-2020 weights of table 21: 688,090 ha). Aragon mean 5,175 EUR/ha (2020: 4,369; +18.5%); Spain 10,248 (+13.8% on 2020). Province-level, includes lowland comarcas outside the footprint; no comarca-level price opened.

**Supporting facts (all opened 2026-10-04 in BOE consolidated text unless noted):**
- Foreigners: Ley 8/1975 art. 4 and RD 689/1978 art. 32 restrict non-EU buyers only in listed zones (islands, Cartagena, the Strait of Gibraltar, the bay of Cadiz, the Portuguese and French frontier zones, Galicia, North Africa); Teruel is not among them; Annex II was read in the verify pass and names no Teruel place. 2025-26 national measures on non-EU buyers: not verified.
- Planning (DL 1/2014, Ley de Urbanismo de Aragon): art. 34.2, isolated single-family house on generic non-urban land only where no nucleus can form (two or more residential buildings in a 150 m radius), one building per parcel, at most 300 m2 built, parcel at least 10,000 m2 tied to it, agrarian or natural use kept; the municipal plan may be stricter. Art. 35.1.c: by special authorisation, rehabilitation of buildings in aldeas, barrios or deserted villages, bordas and torres, with change of use and division into several dwellings if size allows, traditional outer form kept.
- Derecho de abolorio (DL 1/2011, Codigo del Derecho Foral de Aragon, arts. 588-598): relatives to the fourth degree by line of origin may take rustic land and buildings held in the family for two generations; tanteo within 30 natural days, retracto 30 or 90 days, expires two years after sale; it ranks above other pre-emption except co-owners and public bodies (art. 598). Art. 598 bis (Ley 6/2021): vacant immovables pass by law to the Aragonese government. Arts. 584-587: traditional ademprios (pasture, firewood) and mancomunidad.
- Montes: DL 1/2017, texto refundido de la Ley de Montes de Aragon (the former Ley 15/2006): montes de utilidad publica, montes comunales and vecinales en mano comun.
- Comunidad de Albarracin: a local entity of 23 municipalities under Ley 7/1999 (arts. 2.2.e and 95) with statutes of 2007 (BOA no. 31); holds fifteen montes in condominium with the city of Albarracin (standing-notes reading of the statutes). The Comunidad de Aldeas de Teruel did not survive the 1830s.
- Collective vehicles: DL 2/2014 (Ley de Cooperativas de Aragon), SAT, asociacion. Farm leases: Ley 49/2003 art. 12, minimum five years (corrected in the verify pass; an earlier draft said three).
- Programmes: Pueblos Vivos Aragon serves EU nationals and people with Spanish work and residence permits only, no financial aid; Griegos posted on the Bolsa in April 2025 free family housing and EUR 100 a month per school-age child (status for the town hall to confirm). The Bolsa page names no age limit.
- NOT verified: neighbour pre-emption (none in DL 1/2011), Ley 19/1995 and Ley 49/2003 pre-emption; the brief's "Diputacion funding of 29 municipalities"; the Tramacastilla rental figures (7 to 9 homes); municipal plans.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** The law that matters is the municipal plan under DL 1/2014, then who holds the land: a relative with a claim, a Comunidad or a town hall. Rent a village house first and learn the commons rules.
