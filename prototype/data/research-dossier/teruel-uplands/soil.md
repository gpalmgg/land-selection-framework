# Soil and forest — Teruel uplands and the Maestrazgo, Spain (Aragon)

**Headline finding:** Topsoil organic carbon is low to moderate (mean 21.5 g/kg over 0-30 cm), and the forest base is shrinking at about 2.5% of its area per decade net, with one fire-scale year (2009) in a rising trend since 2017.

**Key data point (with vintage):** SoilGrids 2.0 SOC, 250 m, depth-weighted 0-30 cm over the footprint (177,382 pixels): **mean 21.53 g/kg, median 20.17**, p10-p90 15.7-29.1, max 58.3; no pixel above 150. Layer means 43.8 (0-5 cm), 19.6 (5-15), 15.4 (15-30). Hansen GFC v1.11 (2001-2023): forest (canopy 30% or more in 2000) is 16.6% of the footprint (3.13 M pixels, about 1,840 km2); gross loss 6.37% of it (2.77% per decade), gain 0.53%, **net -2.54% per decade**. Computed 2026-10-04.

**Supporting facts:**
- Annual loss, share of year-2000 forest: 2009 1.00% (Aliaga 955 ha, La Zoma 413, Peralejos 214, Villarluengo 198, El Pobo 128); 2010-2016 0.02-0.22%; 2017 0.33%, 2018 0.45%, 2019 0.38%, 2020 0.68%, 2021 0.52%, 2022 0.36%, 2023 0.37%. 2001-2012 total 2.75%, 2013-2023 total 3.62%.
- The Aliaga fire of 2009 burned 7,301 ha, the largest in Spain that year (es.wikipedia, Aliaga). Hansen counts only canopy of 30% or more, so 2,133 ha of loss across the footprint that year is a floor.
- The brief's other fire years do not show: 2012 (284 ha loss in total, Sarrion 40) and 2015 (355 ha, Fuentes de Rubielos 82) are ordinary years; 2022 (768 ha) has no dominant municipality. UNVERIFIED: whether Gobierno de Aragon fire statistics list fires in those years elsewhere.
- Hansen cannot separate fire from harvest; v1.13 (to 2025) exists and was not applied.
- IAEST ficha 2022: forest area burned that year was 0.05 ha (Cuencas Mineras), 7.22 ha (Comunidad de Teruel), 426.63 ha (Jiloca), 0.67 ha (Maestrazgo), 1.97 ha (Sierra de Albarracin), 2.26 ha (Gudar-Javalambre).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** Low carbon here is a reason for slow, grazing- and cover-led soil work, not a verdict. Fire is a landscape fact in these sierras: any plan names the water source, a defensible perimeter and the village's civil-protection arrangements first.
