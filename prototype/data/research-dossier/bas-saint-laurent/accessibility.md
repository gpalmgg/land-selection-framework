# Accessibility — Bas-Saint-Laurent (St. Lawrence south shore), Canada (Québec)

**Headline finding:** About 9 people per square kilometre across the eight MRCs (199,039 residents in 2021 on 22,188 km2 of land area), concentrated in two cities; outside Rimouski and Rivière-du-Loup the density is about 6. The population is older than Québec's and has grown since 2019 through in-migration.

**Key data point (with vintage):** 9.9 p/km2, JRC GHSL POP R2023A 2030 projection (tile R4_C13, 1 km Mollweide), centre-in zonal sum 224,354 people over the 22,667 km2 footprint polygon (inland water included). Alongside, Statistics Canada 2021 Census: 199,039 / 22,188.17 km2 land area = 8.97 p/km2 (sum of the 128 census subdivisions of the eight census divisions, table 98-10-0002-01). GHSL 2020 for comparison: 211,122 (9.3 p/km2), 6 percent above the census count, which is the model, not a census.

**Supporting facts:**
- Census 2021 by MRC (population / land area km2 / p/km2): Kamouraska 21,307 / 2,241 / 9.5; Rivière-du-Loup 35,338 / 1,277 / 27.7; Témiscouata 19,492 / 3,900 / 5.0; Les Basques 8,873 / 1,120 / 7.9; Rimouski-Neigette 57,191 / 2,704 / 21.2; La Mitis 18,363 / 2,276 / 8.1; La Matanie 20,883 / 3,316 / 6.3; La Matapédia 17,592 / 5,355 / 3.3. The cities of Rimouski (48,935) and Rivière-du-Loup (20,118) hold 35 percent of the residents on 1.9 percent of the land; without them the density is 5.97 p/km2.
- Population trend, StatCan table 17-10-0152-01 (July 1 estimates, 2021 boundaries): 203,798 (2001), 197,170 (2019), 199,319 (2021), 204,755 (2025): -3.3 percent 2001-2019, then +3.8 percent 2019-2025 (+2.7 percent 2021-2025). Every MRC grew 2021-2025 (+0.9 percent La Matapédia to +4.9 percent Rimouski-Neigette).
- Components of change, table 17-10-0153-01, 2023/2024 for the eight census divisions: births 1,413; deaths 2,445 (natural decrease 1,032); immigrants 285; net non-permanent residents +1,794; net interprovincial +44; net intraprovincial (from elsewhere in Québec) +747. The peak intraprovincial inflow was 2020/2021 (+1,602).
- Age, 2021 Census (table 98-10-0022-01): 65 and over = 27.6 percent of residents (23.7 percent in 2016; Québec 20.6 percent); median age by MRC 46.8 (Rivière-du-Loup) to 56.8 (Les Basques) versus 43.2 for Québec.
- Nearest hospital: see the hospital-proximity record in data.json (OSM, geodesic, a proxy not a drive time).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** Rimouski (home of a regional hospital, per OSM) and Rivière-du-Loup are the gravity wells; most of the land is at 3 to 8 people per square kilometre. A region that is ageing, shrinking until 2019 and growing again on newcomers has a different welcome logic from one that is empty: there are neighbours, institutions and a labour-short economy to join.
