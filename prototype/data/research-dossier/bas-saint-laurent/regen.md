# Regenerative network — Bas-Saint-Laurent (St. Lawrence south shore), Canada (Québec)

**Headline finding:** No ecovillage, intentional community or permaculture site could be verified within 100 km of the suggested coordinates (-68.9, 47.9), so the cell is 0 sites, labelled as "none verified", not as a claim that none exist. What the region has is a dense welcome and succession infrastructure: support structures, not sites.

**Key data point (with vintage):** 0 named sites within 100 km (haversine from -68.9, 47.9), checked 2026-10-04 against two sources:

**Supporting facts:**
- Limits of the check: 185 GEN profiles carry no map coordinates and cannot be placed (11 of them list Canada: Écocommunauté L'Insolite, A New Day, Forest Gardens, Wandering Coyote Community, Chuckleberry Community Farm, Edgewood Ecovillage, Highgrove Farm & Commons, Pocashack, Le Manoir, Hearthstone Village, Seeds for Eco Communities); 693 profiles listing other countries, including 131 in the United States, were not fetched because the listing names their country (none of the 131 names refers to northern Maine). OSM is volunteer-tagged and patchy in rural Québec. A zero here therefore means "not verified", and the real number is probably above zero. Compare the Eastern Townships cell, which carries 10 sites that its own reverification could not reproduce.
- The brief's expectation was "sparse": this is consistent.
- Support structures, not sites (listed in the notes, not counted): Place aux jeunes (18-35; regional agents in each MRC), the regional welcome and integration services (https://www.bas-saint-laurent.org/fr/setablir/accueil-integration.html), ARTERRE farm-succession matching (https://www.arterre.ca, CRAAQ), and Cultiver le Bas-Saint-Laurent run by the Table de concertation bioalimentaire du Bas-Saint-Laurent (https://www.tcbbsl.org/, "Transférer sa ferme" farm-transfer days, 27 January and 3 February 2026).
- GEN Canada and Ecovillages Canada pages (ecovillagescanada.ca, ecovillage.org/ecovillages/?country=Canada) opened: no Bas-Saint-Laurent listing; the FIC directory search for Rimouski returned no communities.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** A zero in a regenerative-network count is not a verdict on the place. In this region the network is municipal and institutional (agents, succession programmes), and the relationship to build is with those and with the villages, not with an existing ecovillage.
