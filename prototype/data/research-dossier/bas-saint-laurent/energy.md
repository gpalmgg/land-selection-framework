# Energy — Bas-Saint-Laurent (St. Lawrence south shore), Canada (Québec)

**Headline finding:** Moderate solar resource, in line with the other cold northeastern regions: 1,278 kWh/kWp (footprint mean of 315 sample points), 1,299 at the suggested coordinates.

**Key data point (with vintage):** PVOUT long-term average, Global Solar Atlas LTA API (`PVOUT_csi`, Solargis model v2.2.68, period to 2025), 315 sample points on a 0.08 degree latitude by 0.11 degree longitude grid inside the footprint, retrieved 2026-10-04: mean 1,277.8; median 1,278.4; min 1,060.8; max 1,387.0; p10-p90 1,233.3-1,324.8. At (47.9, -68.9): PVOUT 1,299.0, GHI 1,235.6 kWh/m2. Cell value 1,278.

**Supporting facts:**
- The grid sampling is regular in degrees, not equal-area; at 48 N the cells are about 9 km by 8 km. The low tail (1,061) is the deep valley and north-facing sites; the high tail (1,387) is exposed ground near the estuary.
- Grid electricity, net metering and Hydro-Québec tariffs were NOT researched in this run (the criterion is solar potential only). Marked UNVERIFIED if a later page cites them.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** Long winters and snow cover lower real winter yield; the cell is a long-term annual resource number, not a design figure.
