# Stability — Bas-Saint-Laurent (St. Lawrence south shore), Canada (Québec)

**Headline finding:** No UCDP-recorded fatal organised-violence events within 200 km of the suggested coordinates in 2019-2024 (or 2025).

**Key data point (with vintage):** 0 events, UCDP GED v25.1, 2019-2024, within 200 km (haversine, R = 6371.0088 km) of (-68.9, 47.9). Also 0 within 250 km and 300 km. UCDP GED v26.1 (covers 2025): 0 events 2019-2025 within 200 km, 300 km and 500 km. Computed 2026-10-04 from the repository copy of GEDEvent_v25_1.csv (385,918 events 1989-2024) and the v26.1 CSV (417,968 events to 2025).

**Supporting facts:**
- The only GED event within 200 km in any year is one event in 2000 (one death, geocoded 158 km from the coordinates, description "near a ski hill in the Laurentians north of Montreal" so the point is a geocoding artefact); the nearest 1995-1998 Québec City events are 215 km away.
- A 200 km circle from the coordinates reaches New Brunswick and Maine but no events there either.
- The UCDP threshold is organised violence with at least one death; it says nothing about the Indigenous title and fisheries disputes in the neighbouring Gaspésie and New Brunswick, which are political, not armed, conflicts.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** The measure is deliberately blunt; the stability question for this region is institutional (farmland law, language, relationships with the Wolastoqiyik and Mi'gmaq nations), not violence.
