# Water — Bas-Saint-Laurent (St. Lawrence south shore), Canada (Québec)

**Headline finding:** Low projected water stress across the footprint (area-weighted 0.079 on the Aqueduct 0-1 ratio, 2050 business-as-usual), with a Low-medium band in the single western basin that covers 28 percent of the area; water is in Québec law a collective resource of the nation, withdrawals of 75,000 litres a day or more need ministerial authorization.

**Key data point (with vintage):** 0.079 (reported 0.08), WRI Aqueduct 4.0 future_annual `bau50_ws_x_r` (raw 0-1 ratio, business-as-usual 2050), area-weighted over 12 HydroBASINS level-6 basins intersecting the footprint (22,820 km2 covered of 22,822 km2). Min 0.005, max 0.193. Area by Aqueduct category: Low (under 10 percent) 16,496 km2 (72 percent); Low-medium (10-20 percent) 6,325 km2 (28 percent). Baseline (`bws_raw`) area-weighted: 0.060. Aqueduct 4.0 release Aq40_Y2023D07M05 (repository copy), computed 2026-10-04.

**Supporting facts:**
- Basins dominating the footprint by area: 726013 (0.193, Low-medium, 6,325 km2, centroid of the overlap about -69.1, 47.9, the western part of the region), 726087 (0.030, 4,372 km2; the basin at the suggested coordinates), 726040 (0.026, 4,088 km2), 726014 (0.091, 1,821 km2), 726020 (0.005, 1,696 km2), 726016 (0.037, 1,689 km2). The label for the cell is therefore "Low to low-medium", not plain Low.
- Law: An Act to affirm the collective nature of water resources and provide for increased water resource protection (SQ 2009, c. 21, assented 12 June 2009), s. 1: "both surface water and groundwater, in their natural state, are resources that are part of the common heritage of the Québec nation". Environment Quality Act new s. 31.75 (inserted by that Act): withdrawals are subject to the authorization of the Minister, except withdrawals "with a maximum flow rate of less than 75,000 litres per day" (and other exemptions); authorizations are transferable (s. 31.84).
- The same Act's preamble recites the Great Lakes-St. Lawrence River Basin Sustainable Water Resources Agreement (signed 13 December 2005, approved by the National Assembly 30 November 2006); its effect on withdrawals in this footprint was not analysed.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** Low stress at basin scale is not the same as a reliable well: rural supply is typically private wells and municipal systems, and no hydrogeological or well-yield data for the eight MRCs were opened in this run. The Rimouski, Rivière-du-Loup and Matane river systems and the Témiscouata and Matapédia lakes and rivers are the surface-water story; groundwater quality and yields are an unverified gap.
