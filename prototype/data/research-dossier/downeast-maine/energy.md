# Energy — Downeast Maine (Washington County), USA

**Headline finding:** A moderate solar resource for a northern latitude: about 1,350 kWh per kWp a year, with little variation across the county (1,311 to 1,389 over land).

**Key data point (with vintage):** **1,350 kWh/kWp**, Global Solar Atlas v2.7, PVOUT (csi) long-term average, retrieved 2026-10-04. 159 land points on a 0.06 x 0.085 degree grid: mean 1,349.99, median 1,348.4, min 1,310.9, max 1,388.9. Value at the coords 1,349.97 (GHI 1,313.8, elevation 66 m). The 33 coastal-water points average 1,373.9 and are excluded; all 192 points average 1,354.1.

**Supporting facts:**
- Land elevation across the sample is 5 to 222 m.
- The Sunrise County Economic Council lists Rural Energy for America Program funding among its farm and business resources and a 'Power to the People' programme; programme terms were not opened.
- Energy was not otherwise researched: heating fuel, grid access and any local wind or tidal projects are open items.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** PVOUT is a modelled long-term average at the pixel; shading, snow cover on panels and the coast's cloud cover shift real yield. Do not read the figure as a promise for a given roof.
