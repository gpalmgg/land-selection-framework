# Accessibility and Demographics — Downeast Maine (Washington County), USA

**Headline finding:** A very sparsely settled, older county, stable in headcount since 2020 only because in-migration offsets natural decrease, with the nearest hospital 23 km from the coordinates and tertiary care in Bangor.

**Key data point (with vintage):** **5.4 p/km2** (GHSL R2023A, 2030 epoch, 35,522 people over 6,637.44 km2 land area); official 2020 Census 31,095 / 6,637.44 = 4.68 p/km2. Total area 8,437.1 km2 including coastal and inland water (3.7 p/km2 on that basis).

**Supporting facts:**
- Census population 32,856 (2010), 31,095 (2020), estimate 31,383 (1 July 2024); peak since 2020 31,507 (2022). Deaths exceed births (2024: 260 births, 604 deaths); net migration +286 in 2024 (domestic +231, international +55), +590 in 2022. Net out from 2012 to 2018.
- Median age 48.6 and 27.2% aged 65 or over on 1 July 2024, against 23.5% for Maine overall.
- Hospitals (OSM, geodesic from the coords): Down East Community Hospital (Machias) 22.85 km; Calais Regional Hospital 40.43 km; Charlotte County Hospital (St. Stephen, New Brunswick) 41.96 km; Eastern Maine Medical Center (Bangor) 91.27 km. 3 within 50 km and 16 within 100 km (counts include Canadian hospitals). The proxy is not a drive time.
- Farms: 341 (down 10% since 2017); producers 585, of whom 262 are 65 or older and 25 under 35 (USDA 2022).
- Housing: an estimated 59% of households cannot afford to buy a home; a median-priced home in Machiasport costs about seven times typical household earnings (SCEC, June 2026).
- Tribal land: Indian Township Reservation 97.2 km2, Pleasant Point Reservation 1.5 km2, Passamaquoddy trust land about 62 km2 inside the county; together about 2.4% of county land.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** The coordinates are in the town of Wesley, a sparsely settled area, so the distance to Machias is a real rural figure and not a hub-centroid artefact. Roads, winter conditions and broadband were not researched; the Sunrise County Economic Council lists broadband programmes.
