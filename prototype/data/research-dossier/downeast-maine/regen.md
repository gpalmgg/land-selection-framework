# Regenerative Network — Downeast Maine (Washington County), USA

**Headline finding:** No intentional community or ecovillage is listed within 100 km of the county in the directories the tool counts. The regenerative and land-return work here is carried by land trusts, farm organisations and Wabanaki-led institutions, which are named but not counted as sites.

**Key data point (with vintage):** **0 sites** within 100 km of [-67.6, 44.9] (a circle that contains the whole county), retrieved 2026-10-04. **Supporting facts:**
- Directory entries without a map location are not in the marker files; zero means none listed, not none existing.
- Named institutions (not counted): Maine Farmland Trust and its Maine Farm Link service (two Washington County lease listings on 2026-10-04); MOFGA; Maine Coast Heritage Trust (all three are First Light committed organizations); Downeast Lakes Land Trust (Grand Lake Stream, founded 2001, not on First Light's list); the Sunrise County Economic Council (Machias and Calais); the Cobscook Bay Resource Center (Eastport); First Light and the Wabanaki Commission on Land and Stewardship.
- Wabanaki land and food work is not in GEN or FIC and is recorded here, not counted: the Tributary Land Returns (11 projects, over 50,000 acres, five Nations, seven conservation partners, affirmed by the Wabanaki Commission); Kuwesuwi Monihq (Pine Island, Big Lake, 140 acres); the Wolankeyutomone kisi apaciyewik fund; Passamaquoddy tribal ventures at Indian Township including a wild blueberry company and maple.
- The Land Returns are not a place for settlers to buy near; the Commission's and First Light's own process puts Tribal governments' requests first.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** An empty directory is not an empty landscape. Belonging here runs through institutions that already hold relationships with the Nations; the honest first act is to read their guidance and ask.
