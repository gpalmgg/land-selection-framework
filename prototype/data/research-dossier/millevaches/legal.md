# Legal + Economic — Plateau de Millevaches (Limousin), France

**Headline finding:** France sets no nationality bar that the codes opened reveal, but rural land here is not simply an open market: the Safer Nouvelle-Aquitaine can pre-empt agricultural sales and resell them to another buyer, communes and neighbours hold first refusal on small woods, and farmland in hamlet commons (sections de commune) goes first to farmers who live there. Per-hectare prices for free farmland are low (about 2,000 to 3,700 EUR).

**Key data point (with vintage):** Free, unbuilt farmland and meadow, 2025, petites régions agricoles "Plateau de Millevaches": Creuse **2,040 EUR/ha**, Corrèze (Artense, Plateau de Millevaches) **3,070**, Haute-Vienne **3,730**. Departmental means: Creuse 2,890, Corrèze 3,880 (range 930 to 8,000), Haute-Vienne 3,550. Rented land 2,140 to 2,990. Woodland, Massif Central forest region: 2,850 EUR/ha (-0.2 percent on 2024). Confidence: high on the statistics, low on what any single parcel costs (they are averages, not appraisals).

**Supporting facts:**
- Safer pre-emption (Code rural L143-1 et seq.): applies to agricultural buildings and bare land with agricultural vocation; outside any urbanism document, to non-urbanised parts of the commune excluding woods and forests (L143-1); exempt: transfers to relatives to the fourth degree and co-heirs (L143-4); conditions and minimum surface fixed by decree for each Safer (L143-7); the Safer can ask to visit the property and a silence after the deadline is a waiver (L143-8). Which Safer: Safer Nouvelle-Aquitaine, with departmental services for Corrèze, Creuse and Haute-Vienne. Nationally in 2023, 3,750 pre-emptions were about 1 percent of 393,800 notified sales.
- Small woods: under 4 ha, owners of a contiguous wooded parcel have a right of preference, with two months to answer (Code forestier L331-19); the commune has rights in specific cases (L331-22, L331-24).
- Sections de commune (CGCT L2411-1 and following): a part of a commune holding goods or rights of its own; a public-law legal person; managed by the council and mayor; no new section since 2013; agricultural land goes in priority to farmers domiciled and installed on the section (L2411-10). One plateau commune, Faux-la-Montagne, manages a sectional forest (Régeat).
- Planning: of the 124 communes, 59 have no local urbanism document (building only in already urbanised parts, Code de l'urbanisme L111-3), 52 are in a PLUi, 8 in a PLU, 1 in a carte communale; local documents must be compatible with the Parc charter 2018–2033 (Code de l'environnement L333-1). Source: Géoportail de l'Urbanisme through API Carto, 2026-10-04.
- Collective vehicles: GFA (L322-1), GAEC (L323-1), SCIC, association; Terre de Liens buys farms with citizen savings and leases them to farmers who cannot or do not wish to own (Terre de Liens Limousin; Objectif Terres lists farms). Since the 2021 law the Safer is informed of share transfers of land-holding companies (L141-1-1).
- Do not copy the Cévennes "0.7 ha" threshold: it is set by decree per Safer and was not looked up here.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:**
The practical gate is not price but standing: a project the commune, the associations or a Terre de Liens lease can hold passes through the plateau's own channels; a lone purchase of an isolated parcel with no structure behind it is the case this tool answers "not there". Legifrance returns 403 to bots, so the consolidated codes were read through the codes.droit.org mirror (updated 2026-10-01).
