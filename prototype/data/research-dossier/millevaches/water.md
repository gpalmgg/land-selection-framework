# Water — Plateau de Millevaches (Limousin), France

**Headline finding:** This is a source territory. Eleven or more rivers rise here (Vienne, Creuse, Taurion, Maulde, Combade on the Loire-Bretagne side; Vézère, Corrèze, Luzège, Triouzoune, Diège, Chavanon on the Adour-Garonne side), and the Parc says 80 percent of Limousin rivers start on it. Projected scarcity is low to medium.

**Key data point (with vintage):** WRI Aqueduct 4.0, baseline water stress ratio under business-as-usual 2050 (bau50_ws_x_r), area-weighted over four HydroBASINS level-6 basins that intersect the 3,351.7 km2 footprint: **0.174** (min 0.090, max 0.273). By area: Medium-high (20 to 40 percent) 1,776 km2 (53 percent), Low (under 10 percent) 1,576 km2 (47 percent). Two basins dominate: 232160 (1,769 km2, 0.248) and 232240 (1,529 km2, 0.090). Computed 2026-10-04 from the Aqueduct 4.0 geodatabase (future_annual layer).

**Supporting facts:**
- Parc figures: 2.15 km of stream per km2, 6,750 km of watercourse, wetlands about 20 percent of the area (eight times the metropolitan share), peatland 3.4 percent versus 0.2 percent of metropolitan France; every lake and pond on the plateau is artificial.
- The Parc straddles two SDAGE basins, Loire-Bretagne and Adour-Garonne, and states a responsibility toward the downstream for quality and regularity of flow.
- Communes are competent for drinking-water distribution (CGCT L2224-7-1); the prefect can limit uses in drought (Code de l'environnement L211-3); withdrawals up to 1,000 m3 a year count as domestic use (R214-5).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:**
Plentiful water here is also a shared duty: wetlands and peat are the buffer that downstream towns rely on, and building or draining them is the harm the Parc names. The Aqueduct ratio is a regional abstraction-to-supply figure, not a statement about any spring. Which communes run their own supply, which belong to a syndicat, and which hamlets draw on private springs was not enumerated.
