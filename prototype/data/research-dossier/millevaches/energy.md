# Energy — Plateau de Millevaches (Limousin), France

**Headline finding:** Solar potential is moderate, set by latitude and cloud, with little difference between the plateau's sub-areas. Forest (wood) is the plateau's other local energy resource and its main land-use controversy.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Global Solar Atlas long-term average PV output (PVOUT, crystalline silicon), 75-point grid over the footprint (about 6.7 km by 6.5 km spacing): mean **1,256 kWh/kWp/yr**, median 1,255, range 1,208 to 1,296 (p10–p90 1,233–1,279); 1,265 at the coordinates (GHI 1,276 kWh/m2). Retrieved from api.globalsolaratlas.info 2026-10-04.

**Supporting facts:**
- Forest covers about half the Parc (164,000 ha, 55 percent boisement by one Parc figure, "around 50 percent" by another); over 90 percent is private, about 21,000 owners (Parc, forest charter).
- Roughly half the forest is natural hardwood colonisation after farm abandonment; the other half is post-war plantation under the Fonds forestier national: Scots pine first, then spruce, then Douglas-fir, which has dominated plantings since the 1970s (Parc).
- Two thirds of the harvested volume leaves the plateau for processing elsewhere (Parc, forest charter).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:**
Wood is not a neutral fuel here. Protests against "enrésinement" have run for more than forty years, and the Parc's charter treats the forest as both livelihood and contested landscape; a household that burns wood is taking a position in that argument. Roof solar works at roughly the same yield anywhere on the plateau.
