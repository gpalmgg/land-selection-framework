# Stability — Plateau de Millevaches (Limousin), France

**Headline finding:** No UCDP-recorded fatal political violence within 200 km of the plateau in 2019–2024; the nearest event of any year since 1989 is 232 km away. The tensions that matter here are local, social and about land and forest.

**Key data point (with vintage):** UCDP GED v25.1 (1989–2024): **0 events** within 200 km of 45.65 N, 2.0 E for 2019–2024 (also 0 within 250 and 300 km). GED v26.1 (covers 2025): also 0. Nearest events of any year: Toulouse 2012 (232 km), near Carcassonne and Trèbes 2018 (272 to 274 km). Computed 2026-10-04 from the full GED CSVs by haversine distance. The Cévennes reverify found 2 events in its 200 km circle; this circle does not reach them.

**Supporting facts:**
- Documented friction on the plateau: returning native retirees seeking municipal office with interests that often diverge from the councils' (Bobbé, IPNS no. 31, 2010); "rural gentrification" by better-resourced, environment-seeking newcomers, differing values between returnees (heritage) and newcomers (ecology), and a sharp newcomer-versus-local polarisation in the 2014 municipal elections (Richard, Dellier and Tommasi, Revue de géographie alpine 102-3, 2014, read on openedition).
- The forest has been contested for over forty years (Parc, forest charter).
- Population has been stable since 2011 after fifty years of loss (INSEE).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:**
A zero conflict count says nothing about social standing. The documented frictions (returnees and newcomers, the forest, scattered building away from bourgs) are about standing in the commune, so the honest risk is arriving as a lone buyer with no local structure behind you; that reading is this tool's, not a finding of the sources.
