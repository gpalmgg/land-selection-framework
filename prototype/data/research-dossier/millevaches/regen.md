# Regenerative network — Plateau de Millevaches (Limousin), France

**Headline finding:** The directories list few intentional-community or ecovillage projects around the plateau, and none inside the Parc perimeter. The plateau's regenerative life is carried by associations, cooperatives and farm-access structures, which are not ecovillage sites and are listed separately.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Sites within about 100 km of 45.65 N, 2.0 E, retrieved 2026-10-04: **5**, all GEN directory listings: Eco Chateau / Château de Cautine (56 km, forming), L'éco-jardin du Périgord (68 km, status not stated), La Forge du Vallon (93 km, established 2020), Néo-Village Terra Nostra (94 km, established 2018), and the Autonomy project at Tierra del Corazon (about 60 km; located only by its own text, "30 minutes south of Limoges in Limousin"). OpenStreetMap, queried with the repository's ten intentional-community and permaculture tag filters within 100 km: 0. Eurotopia print directory (postal-code search): no entry for postcodes 19, 23, 24, 63, 15; one unnamed entry for 87 (Haute-Vienne), not counted.

**Supporting facts:**
- The GEN directory holds 1,335 community listings, 1,096 with coordinates; 33 are in France; four of them fall inside the 100 km circle by coordinates and one more by its own text.
- Not ecovillage sites, listed for context: Télé Millevaches (rural media begun 1986 by residents of Faux-la-Montagne, Peyrelevade and Gentioux); IPNS (association-printed quarterly); L'Arban (SCIC housing and planning cooperative, 193 members at end 2022, per its own page); Terre de Liens Limousin (8 farms region-wide, citizen savings, only Le Masmoutard at Soubrebost verified inside the Parc).
- Named in the brief but not opened: Ambiance Bois, De fil en réseaux, L'Atelier (Royère-de-Vassivière), La Renouée (Gentioux), La Tournerie (Coussac-Bonneval, Haute-Vienne, outside the Parc).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:**
A low count here describes the directories, not the people. Two of the five listings are projects still recruiting their first residents (one asks for €750,000 across joiners), which is a different thing from a rooted community; read each listing's own terms. For the plateau itself the useful route is through the commune and the associations that already exist.
