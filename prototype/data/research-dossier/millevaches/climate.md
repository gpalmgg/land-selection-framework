# Climate — Plateau de Millevaches (Limousin), France

**Headline finding:** The plateau is a cool granite upland, roughly 300 to 1,000 m, that the CMIP6 ensemble warms by about 2.3 °C between the 1970–2000 normal and 2041–2060. It stays cooler than the Cévennes or the lowland west of it, but its buffers (altitude, wetland, forest) are moderate, not strong.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Mean annual temperature, 2041–2060, SSP2-4.5, 17-GCM mean of WorldClim 2.1 CMIP6 (2.5 arc-minute grid), zonal mean over the 124-commune Parc footprint (284 cells): **12.0 °C** (GCM range 11.1 to 13.4). The 1970–2000 WorldClim normal over the same footprint is 9.7 °C (cell range 8.4 to 11.3). Value at the coordinates (45.65 N, 2.0 E; 3 x 3 cell window): 11.15 °C, cooler than the zonal mean because the point sits near 800 m. Retrieved 2026-10-04.

**Supporting facts:**
- Altitude spread: the Parc gives 300 to 1,000 m; a 75-point Global Solar Atlas grid over the footprint sampled 367 to 913 m, mean 668 m (retrieved 2026-10-04).
- Rainfall: the Parc gives 800 to 1,600 mm a year; wetlands about 20 percent of the area, peatland 3.4 percent (Parc, "Un territoire de sources et de zones humides").
- The Parc notes repeated drought periods and, in its forest charter, lists water resource, fire access and dieback among the climate risks to anticipate.
- GFDL-ESM4 returned 404 on the WorldClim server, so the ensemble is 17 models, as in the repository's re-verification of the other regions.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:**
National services (Météo-France DRIAS, which has no 2041–2060 horizon) are context only and are not relabelled SSP2-4.5 here. A household at 900 m and one in a valley at 450 m live in different climates: the +2.3 °C is an average over a plateau whose floor is cooler than the footprint mean. Precipitation projections and a drought index were not opened and are the gap to close before anyone designs water storage.
