# Accessibility and people — Plateau de Millevaches (Limousin), France

**Headline finding:** One of the least densely settled places in the dataset, 11.6 inhabitants per km2, still losing people through natural decline but roughly holding its population through arrivals; the nearest general hospital is in Ussel, 27 km from the coordinates in a straight line.

**Key data point (with vintage):** INSEE municipal population 2023 for the 124 communes: 38,746 on 3,351.7 km2 = **11.6 p/km2** (Corrèze 65 communes 20,954 / 1,780 km2 = 11.8; Creuse 43 communes 10,930 / 1,117 km2 = 9.8; Haute-Vienne 16 communes 6,862 / 455 km2 = 15.1). JRC GHSL POP R2023A over the same footprint: 2020 = 11.07, 2030 projection = **9.98 p/km2** (1 km Mollweide grid, cell-centre mask). Seven communes above 1,000 inhabitants (Meymac 2,389; Eymoutiers 2,042; Felletin 1,577; Chamberet 1,433; Treignac 1,256; Corrèze 1,171; Peyrat-le-Château 1,025) hold 28 percent of residents; the 117 smaller communes average 9.3 p/km2.

**Supporting facts:**
- Series (sum of the 124 communes): 1968 55,189; 1999 40,515; 2011 39,298; 2016 38,950; 2020 38,564; 2023 38,746 (INSEE, base historique 1876–2023).
- Age: 32.7 percent aged 65 or over versus 20.5 percent across French communes (INSEE RP 2021).
- Hospitals (OSM snapshot, straight-line from the coordinates, not a road isochrone): Centre hospitalier de Haute-Corrèze (Ussel) 27.0 km; Aubusson 37.3 km; Tulle 45.8 km; Limoges university hospital 62.0 km; Brive 64.7 km; 14 OSM hospital points within 50 km and 81 within 100 km, several of them psychiatric or rehabilitation sites.
- Hubs outside the perimeter: Ussel, Égletons, Aubusson, Bourganeuf, Tulle, Limoges.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:**
The coordinates fall in the village of Tarnac, not a hub town, so the 27 km is a fair rural figure, but the plateau's winter roads make straight-line distance optimistic. Low density also means thin services; what arriving households help hold or lose is as much a part of the place as the land.
