# Stability — North Karelia and Kainuu, Finland

**Headline finding:** No recorded armed-conflict event within 200 km of the region coordinates for 2019-2024; the eastern edge of the footprint is the Russian border, which is context and a legal matter (border zone, buyer rules), never a score.

**Key data point (with vintage):** UCDP GED v25.1 (2019-2024) and v26.1 (adds 2025): **0 events within 200 km** of [29.7, 63.3], and also none within 300 km. The farthest point of the footprint is 181.8 km from the coordinates. The nearest event to the footprint polygon in 2019-2024 is 241 km away (Gatchina district, Leningrad region, 2024, one death); the nearest at any date is 204 km (Saint Petersburg, 2009). The 200 km circle does reach into Russian Karelia, where the data record nothing.

**Supporting facts:**
- Border Guard Act 578/2005 (Finlex, consolidated 2025-04-25): the border zone on land is up to 3 km (s.49); staying in it needs a permit (s.52); residents and property-holders get an open-ended permit (s.53); notification is required for felling within 500 m of the border line (s.57).
- Ministry of Defence permit regime for non-EU/EEA buyers, including the 2025 amendment on Russian and Belarusian nationals (see legal.md).
- Democratic stability and rule of law are not scored here; UCDP records organised violence only.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** Land close to the border carries paperwork, not danger statistics: ask for the border-zone map and the Border Guard permit position before committing to a plot near the eastern line (Ilomantsi, Lieksa, Kuhmo, Tohmajärvi, Kitee).
