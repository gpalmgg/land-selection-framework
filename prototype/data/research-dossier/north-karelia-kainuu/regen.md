# Regenerative Ecosystem — North Karelia and Kainuu, Finland

**Headline finding:** Sparse, and said so: one listed community site within 100 km of the coordinates. The regenerative thread here runs through village associations, Leader groups and a rewilding cooperative rather than ecovillages.

**Key data point (with vintage):** **1 site** within 100 km of [29.7, 63.3], retrieved 2026-10-04: **Nomad Town** (NomadTown S.T.O.P. ResilienceHub), outskirts of Joensuu (about 78 km from Joensuu centre), listed by SKEY (GEN Finland, national network of sustainable living communities). The count covers the footprint core only.

**Supporting facts:**
- SKEY's list holds 14 Finnish communities; Nomad Town is the only one in North Karelia or Kainuu. The GEN directory marker file (built 2026-10) holds 5 Finnish communities, the nearest Good Life Community, Rautalampi, 162 km from the coordinates. OpenStreetMap (Overpass snapshot 2026-10-04T14:02Z, 100 km, community/ecovillage/intentional/permaculture tags) returned 0 elements.
- Nomad Town: founded August 2019 on a rented plot of about one hectare by Maaseutuyhdistys Sydänlanka ry; Yle (2019) reported one permanent resident and capacity of about 18; a podcast page reports about 2 to 2.5 residents; the project blog's last post is November 2023. Activity since then is not confirmed.
- Named but not counted: Auroora, Rääkkylä (Kotocoop co-operative housing unit in a former school, 4-5 people, bought 2021) about 111 km from the coordinates, inside the footprint but outside the radius; Snowchange Cooperative, Selkie village, Kontiolahti (about 76 km), a rewilding and traditional-knowledge organisation that restored the 110 ha Linnunsuo wetland; Mekrijärvi in Ilomantsi, a former research station mentioned only in a secondary article.
- Support structures (not sites): Leader groups (Vaara-Karjalan Leader, Joensuun Seudun Leader, Keski-Karjalan Jetina, Elävä Kainuu Leader, Oulujärvi Leader), 25 village associations or committees in Kuhmo alone, Kainuun kylät ry. See the land standing entry.
- Eskola (Eskolan Kyläpalvelu Oy) is in Central Ostrobothnia, not in the footprint.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** A low count is an honest reading, not a gap to fill: arriving here means joining existing village structures, not adding to a movement that is already thick.
