# Legal and Land — North Karelia and Kainuu, Finland

**Headline finding:** Freehold land with no residency requirement and no arrival programme: EU and EEA citizens buy freely, non-EU/EEA buyers need a Ministry of Defence permit (which can be refused to Russian and Belarusian buyers since 15 July 2025), municipalities and the state hold pre-emption rights, and shores are built only under a plan. Everyman's rights are access rights, not occupation rights.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Forest-only unbuilt properties over 2 ha, **median price 2,869 to 6,279 EUR/ha by municipality in 2025** (Ristijärvi lowest, Kitee highest; 433 sales, sale-weighted mean of municipal medians 4,698); counties North Karelia 5,191 (321 sales) and Kainuu 2,969 (180 sales). National Land Survey purchase price register, representative sales, retrieved 2026-10-04. Direction of regulation: tightening (2022-2025).

**Supporting facts:**
- Foreign ownership: Act on Permit Requirements for Certain Real Estate Acquisitions 470/2019 ss.1-2: a permit is required when the buyer is a person who is not a citizen of an EU or EEA state, or an entity seated outside the EU/EEA, or an EU/EEA entity in which such a person holds one tenth or more of the votes; no permit when the seller is a spouse or someone the buyer could inherit from, or spouses buy together and one does not need a permit. The Ministry may grant the permit unless the acquisition would hinder defence, territorial surveillance, border control or security of supply (s.5). Amendment adopted 11 April 2025, in force 15 July 2025: the Ministry may prevent acquisitions by individuals or entities whose state of nationality is waging a war of aggression; the Ministry names Russia and Belarus among the states that meet the criteria (decree in force 15 July 2025); permanent residence permits are taken into account.
- Residency: nothing in the Act makes residency a condition of buying; EU/EEA buyers need no permit.
- Collective routes: jointly owned forest (yhteismetsä, Act 109/2003 s.1-5; the shareholders' association is a legal entity and shareholders are not personally liable); joint-area associations (osakaskunta, Act 758/1989 ss.1-3); co-operatives and housing companies are used locally but their statutes were not opened.
- Pre-emption: municipality (Act 608/1977 s.1; not for 5,000 m2 or less, spouse or heir buyers, or forced auctions); state (Act 469/2019: within 1,000 m of defence and Border Guard areas and facilities; municipality and parish buyers are exempt).
- Planning gate: building permit for any new dwelling or building of 30 m2 or more (Building Act 751/2023 s.42, in force 1 Jan 2025); shore zone building needs a detailed plan or a master plan that expressly serves as a permit basis (Land Use Act 132/1999 s.72), with exceptions for farming, forestry and fishing buildings and small additions; planning-need areas (s.16).
- Border: border zone up to 3 km on land, staying needs a permit, open-ended for residents and property-holders (Border Guard Act 578/2005 ss.49-53).
- Ownership: private owners hold about 53.5% of North Karelia's forest land and 40.1% of Kainuu's, the state (Metsähallitus) 19.6% and 42.8%, companies 22.0% and 12.9% (Luke NFI 13/14, whole counties).
- Prices: cultivated land only, unbuilt: North Karelia median 4,850 EUR/ha (25 sales), Kainuu 2,312 (8 sales); Luke NUTS2 Northern and Eastern Finland agricultural land 6,013 EUR/ha in 2025. Unbuilt shore holiday plots in sparsely populated areas, median 2.62 EUR/m2 in North Karelia (4 sales) and 2.00 in Kainuu (5 sales), 2025.
- Trajectory: sale-weighted mean of footprint forest medians 4,330 (2023), 4,266 (2024), 4,698 (2025); county medians 2021 to 2025 rose 32% (North Karelia) and 39% (Kainuu).
- Exclusions: the footprint omits the Sámi homeland (Act 974/1995 s.4) and the reindeer herding area (Act 848/1990 ss.2-3: Lapland, and in Kainuu Suomussalmi, Hyrynsalmi and part of Puolanka); the honest answer there is not there.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** The permit is a nationality question, not a residency question, and the border-zone and pre-emption rules are location questions: check the plot against the Defence and Border Guard areas before an offer. The agricultural price series is thin (8 to 25 sales a county), so treat it as texture, not a benchmark.
