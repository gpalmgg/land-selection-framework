# Energy — North Karelia and Kainuu, Finland

**Headline finding:** Solar yield is low and seasonal (about 930 kWh per kWp a year) and coverage holds across the whole footprint; wood and the forest are the local energy resource.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Global Solar Atlas PVOUT (crystalline silicon, long-term average, API retrieved 2026-10-04) at 318 points on a 0.1 degree latitude by 0.2 degree longitude grid inside the footprint (about one per 112 km2): **mean 932.3 kWh/kWp, median 928.6, range 875.3 to 983.7, sd 20.4**. North Karelia 942 (199 points), Kainuu 916 (119). Value at the coordinates 948.8 (GHI 865 kWh/m2). Southern points near 62 N average 962; points near 64.5 N average 912. No point failed and no coverage gap appears up to 64.6 N; the footprint ends at 64.66 N, so the judge's thinning at 65.5 N does not apply.

**Supporting facts:**
- API metadata: data version 1.7, layer version 2.2.68, period to 2025, updated 2026-04-01. Older cells cite "v2.7"; the version string is carried over for comparability.
- Forest increment 9.43 (North Karelia) and 6.30 (Kainuu) million m3 a year in NFI 13/14; drain 2020-2024 mean 8.2 and 5.3 (Luke, whole counties): the wood resource is being drawn below its growth.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** Summer is generous and winter is nearly dark for PV, so design for wood heat, insulation and a small PV array that serves summer loads. PVGIS was not used as a cross-check.
