# Soil — North Karelia and Kainuu, Finland

**Headline finding:** Organic, peat-influenced soils: topsoil carbon is very high and 3% of the footprint exceeds the 150 g/kg slider maximum, so the number is a signal about bog and forest floor, not about farmland fertility.

**Key data point (with vintage):** SoilGrids 2.0 soil organic carbon, 0-30 cm depth-weighted (5, 10 and 15 cm layers), 250 m cells inside the footprint (473,014 land cells, 29,563 km2): **median 114.6 g/kg, mean 115.9, p10 96.2, p90 137.6**, maximum 251.4; 3.3% of cells above 150 g/kg, 84% above 100. North Karelia median 111.1, Kainuu 122.2; municipal medians run from 102.5 (Liperi) to 124.3 (Kajaani). Layer means: 0-5 cm 269, 5-15 cm 141, 15-30 cm 48 g/kg. Value at the exact coordinates is a water cell (no data). Retrieved 2026-10-04 from files.isric.org (VRT).

**Supporting facts:**
- Peatlands cover 32.3% of North Karelia's forestry land and 45.0% of Kainuu's (Luke NFI 13/14, 2020-2024, whole counties; national 35.2%).
- The cell value stays inside the 0-150 range and is not clamped; the share above 150 is stated in the cell comment.
- Contamination regime: Environmental Protection Act 527/2014 ch.14 s.133 puts cleaning on the polluter, then on an area holder who consented or knew or should have known of the state of the area when acquiring it, then on the municipality. The national MATTI system holds about 29,000 areas and over 7,000 remediation decisions (ymparisto.fi).
- Legacy: exhausted copper mine at Outokumpu (deposit found 1908); Talvivaara-Terrafame nickel-zinc mine at Sotkamo (opened 2008, first operator bankrupt, Terrafame established 2015); gold-mining zone in preparation at Ilomantsi.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** SoilGrids is a global model and its organic-layer values in boreal forest and peat are the least certain part of the product; use the figure as a flag for "organic, wet, build light", and use the Geological Survey of Finland peat maps before any design. (GTK peat maps were not opened here.)
