# Regenerative network — Finger Lakes (Cayuga and Seneca lake country), United States

**Headline finding:** Nine communities listed in the Foundation for Intentional Community (FIC) and Global Ecovillage Network (GEN) directories fall within 100 km of the coordinates (-76.75, 42.55): one established ecovillage anchor (EcoVillage at Ithaca), two more ecovillage or sanctuary listings, one land co-operative, two small Trumansburg households, and three urban collective houses in Syracuse. OpenStreetMap adds none. The count is a floor, not a census.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Rule applied (docs: upgrade-2026-10/regions/method.md): GEN directory plus OpenStreetMap sites within 100 km (haversine, R = 6371.0088 km) of the coordinates; every site named; programmes, funders and support structures are not counted. Directory read 2026-10-04.
Located by map pin (FIC pin; GEN pin where also listed):
1. EcoVillage at Ithaca, 20.4 km (FIC, 42.45071, -76.54081; also GEN, 20.9 km).
2. White Hawk Ecovillage, 29.3 km (FIC, 42.36291, -76.49861).
3. Via Lactea Sanctuary, 48.7 km (FIC, 42.4145, -76.18487).
4. Common Place Land Cooperative, Inc., 64.0 km (FIC, 42.71248, -75.99936).
5. Bitternut Homestead Urban Collective (Syracuse), 72.5 km (FIC, 43.04396, -76.16916; the OSM way "Bitternut Collective Permaculture Kitchen Garden", 72.5 km, is the same place and is counted once).
6. Bread and Roses Collective (Syracuse), 74.1 km (FIC).
7. 560 Art Collective (Syracuse), 75.2 km (FIC).
Located by listing text (owner hides the map pin; location line reads "New York, United States"):
8. Hitching Post House, a communal house "in the village of Trumansburg, New York" (FIC).
9. Sylvan Oasis, "a 7-acre property at the edge of the village of Trumansburg, in the Finger Lakes region" (FIC).
**Total: 9** (6 outside Syracuse).

**Supporting facts:**
- Method: all 1,185 FIC community pages (ic.org/directory-sitemap1-58, 2026-10-04) and all 1,134 GEN community pages (ecovillage.org/map/community) were fetched and the first map marker read; 496 FIC and 291 GEN listings carry no pin. The 496 FIC no-pin pages were read for a location line: 11 say "New York" (Atelier Farm is on Lake Champlain; Swamp Dog Hobble, Strat House and Youtopia are in Brooklyn; WSTK is near Woodstock; Triple Green Jade Farm Cooperative is Adirondack/Champlain; Geodesic Domes Galore, Mawuni Farm and Roots of Love give no place) so only Hitching Post House and Sylvan Oasis could be placed. The 291 GEN no-pin listings were not located (page structure differs); treat the count as a lower bound.
- OpenStreetMap (Overpass, 2026-10-04T17:56Z and 18:16Z): zero elements within 100 km tagged community=intentional or ecovillage, place or amenity=ecovillage, intentional_community, residential=cohousing, land_use=permaculture, farm=permaculture, permaculture, or the GEN network tags. A name-regex query timed out (HTTP 504) on three attempts, so name-only OSM features are unchecked; the site's own on-disk OSM layer has one point inside 100 km (the Bitternut garden, counted above).
- EcoVillage at Ithaca (EVI): three cohousing neighbourhoods: FROG (30 homes, completed 1997, "first cohousing project in New York State"), SONG (30 homes, completed 2006), TREE (40 households, first move-in October 2013, complete Fall 2015); 170 acres, "over 80%" open green space including 50 acres in a conservation easement; about 170 adults and 40 children (about 210 people). Founded 1991 per its home page. Anyone intending to live at EVI more than 30 consecutive days "must complete the membership process".
- Groundswell Center for Local Food and Farming (programme, not counted): Incubator Farm started 2012, a quarter-acre plot per farmer, on land leased from EcoVillage Ithaca (100 Rachel Carson Way); fees $130-600 per season; applications for 2026 closed, priority to people of color, immigrants, refugees, women, trans and non-binary people.
- Finger Lakes Land Trust (support structure, not counted): founded 1989, 35,000+ acres protected, 45+ nature preserves, about 1,000 additional acres a year (FLLT home page).
- Black Oak Wind Farm: unverified (site did not resolve); dropped.
- The 100 km circle also reaches Syracuse (74.1 km), Binghamton (84.8 km) and Rochester (97.2 km) by straight line. The rule says count them if listed: three Syracuse entries are, and no pinned FIC or GEN listing falls in the Binghamton or Rochester parts of the circle (every pinned listing within 130 km is in the list above or is Community_ARK_Project at 129.7 km, outside).

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** One anchor community does most of the work in this count. The three Syracuse entries are urban collective houses, which the rule counts because FIC lists them as communities; a reader who wants only rural land-based communities should read the number as 6 (and two of those are single households).
