# Energy — Finger Lakes (Cayuga and Seneca lake country), United States

**Headline finding:** Moderate solar resource: Global Solar Atlas long-term average photovoltaic output (PVOUT) averages 1,268 kWh/kWp/yr over the five-county footprint (range 1,203-1,326), inside the 1,200-1,300 range of the Vermont dossier and well below the American Southwest.

**Key data point (with vintage):** Global Solar Atlas v2.7 (Solargis / World Bank; api.globalsolaratlas.info/data/lta), annual PVOUT_csi at 133 grid points (0.06 deg lat x 0.085 deg lon spacing) falling inside the footprint polygon, retrieved 2026-10-04: **mean 1,268.5, median 1,268.0, min 1,203.5, max 1,326.2, p10 1,248.5, p90 1,293.6 kWh/kWp**. At the coordinates (-76.75, 42.55): PVOUT 1,296.2, GHI 1,344.8 kWh/m2, elevation 424 m. A judge's earlier reading of 1,287 at 42.5N, -76.7 sits inside this spread.

**Supporting facts:**
- The sample points span elevations of 73 to 607 m (mean 291 m); PVOUT varies with slope, shading and elevation, so the footprint mean is a regional figure, not a site yield.
- Net-metering rules, community solar programmes and wind projects (the brief names Black Oak Wind Farm) were not opened; blackoakwind.com did not resolve and nothing about it is asserted here.

**Practitioner-relevant nuance:** Winter heating load, not PV yield, sets the design envelope in a cool-temperate climate (the same logic as the Vermont dossier). Confirm the local utility's interconnection terms before assuming grid-tied export.
