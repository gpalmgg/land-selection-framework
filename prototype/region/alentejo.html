What it asksMeet the statutory fire-fuel management duty around buildings and respect the water allocation (Alqueva contract or ARH borehole authorisation) that govern a montado property

Where the answer is not here

  • Buying land classified RAN to build a home with no farm holding behind it: the decree makes RAN areas non aedificandi, forbids building except for the uses it lists, and lists a home only for a farmer on a holding or an owner in proven economic need.

Basis: Decreto-Lei 73/2009 (RAN), arts. 20 to 22 (Diário da República) (opens in a new tab)

What living here asks of you

Alentejo asks you to design from the rainwater equation backward: 500 to 650 mm a year, nearly all falling between October and April, means cisterns sized for six dry months and shade treated as infrastructure, not scenery. Rules under the Reserva Agrícola Nacional (Decreto-Lei 73/2009) let RAN-classified agricultural parcels change hands freely while often forbidding residential construction outright, so verify RAN and REN status and any water rights before buying, not after. The living context is the cork-oak montado with its shared water and fire discipline, thirty years of Tamera nearby, and sparse ageing villages where rooting in matters more than fencing off a herdade.

Source: Decreto-Lei 73/2009, Reserva Agrícola Nacional (RAN), arts. 21-22 (opens in a new tab)

The way in · qualitative · not legal advice

What lawful entry involves, in order

Who may hold
The Civil Code treats foreigners as equal to nationals in civil rights unless a law says otherwise (with a reciprocity clause for states that deny Portuguese citizens the same right); the sources opened found no nationality bar on owning rural land. A non-resident foreigner who wants a Portuguese tax number (NIF) can appoint a fiscal representative, a person or company with tax residence or seat in Portugal. Código Civil art. 14 (consolidated, Diário da República); gov.pt service page 'Nomear representante fiscal' (updated 15.10.2025)
How a group can hold
Cooperativa agrícolaAssociação
  • Cooperativa agrícola. Agricultural co-operatives have their own legal regime (Decreto-Lei 335/99), alongside the general Cooperative Code (Lei 119/2015). Decreto-Lei 335/99, de 20 de agosto
  • Associação. Civil Code chapter on legal persons applies to associations without an economic aim for their members (art. 157 ff.). Código Civil art. 157 (consolidated, Diário da República)
First claim
Owners of adjoining rural parcels smaller than the local unidade de cultura hold a reciprocal right of first refusal when a neighbouring parcel is sold to someone who is not an adjoining owner (Civil Code art. 1380); it does not apply where the land is part of an urban property or is not used for cultivation, or where the sale covers a family-type farm made of dispersed parcels (art. 1381).
Direction
Lei 56/2023 (October 2023) ended new applications for the real-estate-linked investment residence routes it names; the land-ownership rules themselves were not found to have changed.tightening · as of 2026
  1. Find out before any offer whether the parcel lies in the RAN or the REN, because both limit new construction.
    Who decides: Câmara municipal (planning department) of the municipalityDecreto-Lei 73/2009 arts. 21-22 and Decreto-Lei 166/2008 art. 20
  2. Ask the câmara in writing for prior information (pedido de informação prévia) on whether the intended operation is viable and which legal restrictions apply to the parcel.
    Who decides: Câmara municipalDecreto-Lei 555/99 (RJUE) art. 14
  3. If the parcel is in the RAN, a non-agricultural use needs the binding prior opinion of the regional RAN entity, and a home is allowed only as a farmer's permanent residence on the holding or for owners in proven economic need, one use only.
    Who decides: Regional RAN entity, requested directly or through the câmaraDuration: The regional RAN entity issues its opinion within 25 days, and the period is suspended while it asks for further documents Decreto-Lei 73/2009 art. 23(1) and (3)Decreto-Lei 73/2009 arts. 22-23
  4. Check whether owners of adjoining parcels hold a right of first refusal on the sale before signing.
    Who decides: Owners of adjoining rural parcelsCódigo Civil arts. 1380-1381 (consolidated)
  5. A foreign buyer who does not live in Portugal and needs a NIF appoints a fiscal representative and requests the number.
    Who decides: Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira (Finanças), through a fiscal representative resident or seated in Portugalgov.pt, 'Nomear representante fiscal' (updated 15.10.2025)
  6. Choose the holding body for the group, for example an agricultural co-operative or a non-profit association, before the purchase deed.
    Who decides: The founding groupDecreto-Lei 335/99 and Código Civil art. 157
What the law rules out
  • Buying rural land does not by itself carry a right to build: in the RAN and the REN construction is prohibited except for the listed uses.
  • Subdividing or building in the REN is prohibited unless compatible with the protection aims set out in Decreto-Lei 166/2008 art. 20.
Honest gaps
  • Water: whether Alqueva-scheme allocations transfer with a parcel, and the licence needed for a borehole outside the scheme, were not covered by an opened primary source.
  • Processing times for residence permits (the dossier cites 12-18 months as of 2024) were not found in an opened source and are left out.
  • Municipal master plans (PDM) differ by câmara and were not read for Odemira, Beja, Évora or Mértola.
  • Other residence routes (D7, D8, D2) were not opened and are not described.
  • The statutory fire fuel-management duty around buildings (Decreto-Lei 82/2021) was opened but its distances were not extracted.

Where legal and cost questions come first

These are the questions a community has to be able to answer in a place: who may hold the land, how a group can hold it together, and what it costs. Read them as context for a conversation with the people already there, not as a verdict on the place.

Foreign ownership
allowed The Civil Code gives foreigners the same civil rights as nationals unless a law says otherwise (art. 14, with a reciprocity clause); the sources opened found no nationality bar on owning rural land. A foreigner living outside Portugal who needs a tax number (NIF) appoints a fiscal representative.

Several households on one holding

Residency needed to buy
no residency required
Where building is decided
Câmara municipal decides a dwelling parcel by parcel; in the Reserva Agrícola Nacional (RAN) new construction is prohibited except for listed uses (Decreto-Lei 73/2009 arts. 21-22) and in the Reserva Ecológica Nacional (REN) building is prohibited unless compatible with the protection aims (Decreto-Lei 166/2008 art. 20)
First-claim holders
Owners of adjoining rural parcels smaller than the local unidade de cultura (Civil Code art. 1380; not where the land is urban or not cultivated, art. 1381)
Key restriction
RAN and REN parcels can be bought freely but new construction on them is restricted to listed uses, so buying land does not imply a right to build.
Legal direction
tightening Lei 56/2023 (October 2023), art. 42, stopped new applications for the real-estate-linked investment residence routes it names; the land-ownership rules themselves were not found to have changed.

Who made this

The framework was originated by Askja and is developed by a small working group of practitioners and researchers. The demonstration site was built by one member of that group, who holds the practitioner seat.

Members are named by first name and role only, as in the working documents; no surnames, handles or contact details appear anywhere on the site.

There is no public contact route yet; the working group will set one.

How to read it

  • Thresholds filter; they never score or rank.
  • Land standing is qualitative and never a filter.
  • Every value carries its source and vintage.
  • The rivers and contours drawn on this page are ornament, not surveyed. For geography, use the map and ask the people who live there.
